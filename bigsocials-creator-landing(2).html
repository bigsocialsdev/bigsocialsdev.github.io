Questions creators actually ask

Fair question.

No mysterious creator-club rules. Here's what you need to know before you join.

Do I need a certain follower count?

No fixed “10K minimum” gate is the point. Matching considers your niche, audience and engagement alongside follower count, so smaller creators can still be a strong fit for the right brief.

Do I have to pay to join?

No. Creator signup is free. You connect your Instagram and can be considered for relevant paid opportunities without paying an upfront membership fee.

Do I have to negotiate with every brand?

BigSocials is designed to reduce the awkward back-and-forth. Campaign terms are defined in the brief, so you can focus on deciding whether the opportunity is worth your time.

How fast do I get paid?

Once you've fulfilled the brief and the submission is approved, the payout is released from Razorpay escrow. The exact timing depends on the campaign workflow and payment processing.

What if a brand doesn't pay me?

That's exactly why escrow exists. The campaign payment is held through Razorpay escrow, so completing the agreed brief doesn't leave you relying on a brand's promise to pay later.
